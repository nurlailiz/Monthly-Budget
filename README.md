# 🐾 Money Diary — Monthly Budget

A simple phone-friendly budget app that copies the *Monthly Payment Budget* spreadsheet, but is easier to fill in.

**Open `index.html` in any browser.** Nothing to install.

## What it does
- **Month** tab: income, expenses, balance and "still to pay" for each month. Tap ○ to mark a payment as paid ✓. Tap a row to edit it, or tap **+** to add one. Swipe left or right to change month.
- **Summary** tab: every month with income, expenses, balance, still to pay and running savings, plus totals. Tap a month to open it.
- **More** tab: add the next month (fixed items copy over automatically), back up and restore your data, export to CSV, and change the currency.

Your data is saved on your device, in the browser. Use **Download backup** now and then.
